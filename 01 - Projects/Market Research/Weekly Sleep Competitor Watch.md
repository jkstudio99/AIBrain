## What changed / what to monitor
1. **Seasonal promo window**: Emma Mother’s Day push and NITORI 得利週 end around 5/10–5/11; monitor whether competitors extend promos into 618 or switch to Father’s Day messaging.
2. **Trial policy escalation**: Emma/Sleepy Tofu 100-night, IKEA 365-day, HOLA 100-day in-store, Derek 10-day trial + 15-year warranty. For wellness devices/accessories, a 14–30 day local trial may now be table-stakes for trust.
3. **Cooling/anti-humidity claims are everywhere**: 石墨烯, 涼感, TENCEL, 天絲, 抗菌防蟎, moisture/heat management. Any Thai wellness/sleep product should localize around Taiwan humidity and summer sleep discomfort.
4. **Personalization is becoming the wedge**: Dcard discussion favors brands that assess body/sleep posture and solve pain points. HOLA/Good Dreams/EDS-type consultative experiences are raising expectations beyond “soft vs hard.”
5. **Lower-commitment models emerging**: Good Dreams rental/try-at-home snippets and Sleepy Tofu’s LINE/store ecosystem show potential for “try before big purchase.” Track whether mattress rental becomes a wider trend for renters/new movers.
6. **Watch list next week**: Emma 618 lead-up, Sleepy Tofu social/KOL posts around rental/new mover themes, Derek store events or local mall activations, HOLA bedMATCH push, NITORI point-back or markdown cadence, Dcard/PTT complaints around returns and support.

## Recommendations for Lunio / Thai wellness brand
- **Positioning wedge**: อย่าแข่งกับ mattress brands ตรง ๆ; วาง Lunio เป็น “睡前泰式放鬆儀式 / Thai recovery ritual before sleep” สำหรับ office workers, people with neck/shoulder/back tension, parents, and wellness-conscious buyers.
- **Trust package for Taiwan launch**: Traditional Chinese landing page + LINE OA support + official store badge + 14/30-day trial + clear warranty/repair/return process. Trial terms must be simpler than mattress brands.
- **Messaging to test**: “下班後 10 分鐘，讓身體進入好睡狀態,” “不換床，也能升級睡前放鬆,” “泰式舒緩 × 台灣濕熱夜晚.” Avoid medical claims like treating insomnia, pain, or disease unless legally verified.
- **Channel play**: Start with D2C landing + LINE coupon + Shopee/momo official listing for price trust; add KOL demos on IG/Reels/YouTube Shorts. Later test pop-ups with mattress stores, yoga/physio studios, coworking spaces, and wellness cafes.
- **Partnership targets**: Good Dreams/consultative local shops may be more open to “sleep ritual” bundles than IKEA/HOLA; Derek/Sleepy Tofu could be approached later for co-promo only if product quality and localized service are ready.
- **2-week MVP experiment**: Run 2 creatives: (A) office neck/shoulder recovery before sleep, (B) humid-summer sleep relaxation. Landing page offers LINE consultation + early-bird bundle. Success metric: ≥3–5% landing-to-LINE add, ≥1–2% pre-order/lead deposit, CAC below target margin. Stop/pivot if comments question credibility, safety, or after-sales more than they ask price.

## What changed / what to monitor
1. **Promo clock**: Derek และ NITORI campaigns end 5/10–5/11; Emma Mother’s Day/flash sale likely to bridge into **618**. Monitor whether discounts deepen or switch from “mom gift” to “summer cooling”.
2. **Cooling as default claim**: graphene, AirGrid, Cookuru, TENCEL, N COOL, SmartCool, 涼感/恆溫 are now table stakes. New wellness entrant must own a sharper ritual/result angle, not just “cooling”.

- **Position away from mattress replacement**: ใช้ข้อความ “不換床，也能升級睡前放鬆” / “睡前10分鐘泰式舒緩儀式” เพื่อเป็น companion product ไม่ใช่คู่แข่ง mattress.

- **Partnership wedge**: approach consultative local shops/studios first (EDS-like sleep assessment, physio/yoga/wellness studios) before big retailers; pitch as “pre-sleep ritual bundle” not mattress accessory discount.
